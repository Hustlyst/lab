Calendar Horoscope

The shape of your week says who you are. Drop in your calendar and get your type, your lucky hour and what to avoid. For entertainment, with real numbers underneath.

Works offline. Your file is read in this browser and never uploaded.

Drop a calendar file hereGoogle Calendar on a computer: Settings, Import & export, Export, then unzip the download. Outlook desktop: File, Save Calendar. Apple Calendar on a Mac: pick a calendar, File, Export, Export. Or click to choose.

Reads working days only (Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00). Counts meetings with two or more people.

Your reading appears here.

Runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded and there is no tracking.
